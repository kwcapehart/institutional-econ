Why Deliver Slides as a Web Page?

  • Instructors can jump to any slide when questions change the planOpen Menu (M) to choose a slide by title.
  • Study view puts every slide on one scrolling pageStudents can search, print, or read it on a phone.
  • Screen readers get headings, controls, and diagram descriptionsThese accessibility features are built into the file.
  • Students can test themselves with questions and flip cardsThey can revisit difficult material at their own pace.

Labor as a Produced Means of Production

  • Macroeconomics tends to treat labor as an exogenous “given”It focuses on employment, unemployment, and participation.
  • Feminist economics argues that labor is itself producedThe capacity to work must be renewed daily and across generations.
  • Renewal depends on paid and unpaid laborPrivate, public, household, and not-for-profit sectors supply this work.
  • Without renewal, capabilities are depletedWell-being and output decline (see Rai 2024, Depletion).

Key Terms: Flip in Any Order

The Renewal Cycle

Text description: paid and unpaid work feeds the renewal of capabilities. Renewed capabilities make labor available for work. That labor produces output and well-being, which in turn supports more paid and unpaid work, so the cycle repeats. If renewal is not sustained, capabilities are depleted.

The Case for the Web Page

  • Before class, students skim the study viewThey can read on any device and arrive with questions.
  • In class, the timer and slide menu help you respond to the roomYou can change the sequence instead of following a fixed order.
  • After class, students retest themselves with flip cards and quizzesThey can practice at their own pace on any device.
  • You update one file and the link stays the sameYou must host the file yourself; live co-editing is unavailable.
